About UniMates

UniMates is a student-founded platform dedicated to making student living more creative, safe, and community-driven. We believe that finding the right flatmate is about more than just a room—it's about building a home and lasting friendships.


Our mission: Empower students to connect, create, and thrive together.

Our Achievements

Milestones that define our journey

November 2024

EEC 2024 — 1st Place

UniMates claimed first place at the Entrepreneurship & Ecosystem Conference 2024, competing against 40+ student startups from across Greece. The judges praised our innovative approach to student housing and the maturity of our product vision.

1st Place
Top Cohort
March 2025

Piraeus Bank Incubator — Selected Cohort

We were selected among the top teams to join the Piraeus Bank Innovation Lab incubator programme, gaining access to mentorship, funding opportunities, and a network of industry leaders committed to scaling student-focused technology.

June 2025

Reaching 1 000 Active Users

Within six months of launching our beta, UniMates crossed the 1 000 active users milestone — a testament to the genuine need for a safer, community-driven student housing platform in Greece.

These are just some of the highlights from our story so far. Every step of the way, our community has been our greatest achievement.

September 2025

Press Coverage & Media Recognition

UniMates was featured in three major Greek tech and news outlets, bringing our mission to a national audience. The coverage helped us onboard partner universities and sparked conversations about student welfare reform across the country.

5 Partnerships
January 2026

5 University Partnerships

We formalised partnerships with five universities across northern Greece, embedding UniMates into their student services portals and giving thousands of incoming students a trusted first step toward finding their new home.

The road ahead is full of exciting milestones. Stay tuned as we continue building the future of student living.
